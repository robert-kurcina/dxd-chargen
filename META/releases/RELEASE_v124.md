# RELEASE v124 — Armor Abstraction and Sectional Detail

- Refined the Forge Personal Armor editor to match DXD's abstraction-first play philosophy: use detail when the fiction needs it; avoid mandatory Hit Location decomposition when it does not.
- Marked all seven canonical Armor Sets as abstract quick-picks. Their listed Hit Locations are GM guidance rather than a claim that each set has one hidden exact sectional recipe.
- Opening **Customize Armor by Component** no longer immediately destroys the selected quick-pick. The canonical Armor Set remains mechanically authoritative while the player/GM merely opens detailed mode.
- The first actual Sectional Armor edit converts the Suit to a non-canonical custom Suit and retains the originating Armor Set as comparison/provenance guidance.
- Removed user-selection of Light/Medium/Heavy/Field from custom sectional armor. Custom Armor capability is derived from bodily coverage plus calculated D/AR.
- Protection bands are anchored to the canonical Armor Set values: Medium begins at D 4 / AR 12, Heavy at D 6 / AR 18, and Field at D 8 / AR 24 when Field construction also applies. Lower protection remains Light.
- Coverage caps the protection band: torso protection can produce at most Medium capability; Heavy and Field require full Front Torso, Back Torso, both Arms, and both Legs. This allows a very strong breastplate to remain at best Medium while uncovered locations can still receive no Suit protection when optional Hit Locations matter.
- Detailed custom armor now retains separate Front and Rear abstract D/AR summaries so asymmetric armor can be represented without making Hit Locations mandatory in ordinary play.
- Added a **Field construction** control for integrated/custom-fitted full-body construction. It is a classification condition, not a new Trait.
- Helmet, Shield, and Gear selectors are also visible in detailed mode so the third workflow expands the first two instead of acting as an unrelated editor.
- SIZ scaling and aggregate Weight/Cost calculations remain active for all Personal Armor selections.
- No new inferred Sectional Armor catalogue entries were added in this release. Missing Fur/Padded/Quilted/etc. component variants remain for the next decomposition iteration rather than being invented prematurely.
- Character archives were intentionally not normalized; that remains deferred until the revised Armor editor stabilizes.
