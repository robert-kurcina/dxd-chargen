# RELEASE v125 — Sectional Armor Vocabulary and Suggested Decomposition

- Continued the Forge Personal Armor decomposition work without changing the character archives or book-rewrite archive.
- Added a normalized `materialClass` distinction so Fur, Padded, and Quilted are constructions within mechanical **Soft** armor; Metal sectional armor maps to the **Plate** material family.
- Expanded Sectional Armor from 30 canonical entries to 67 entries by adding 37 derived catalogue candidates. The additions fill material/form gaps for Soft, Boiled, Reinforced, and Mail constructions using the page-187 Armor Composition material ladder plus the nearest canonical component form.
- Added full-torso Soft sectional forms: `Jacket, Fur`, `Jacket, Padded`, and `Jacket, Quilted`. Quilted is the default detailed interpretation of the abstract `Armor Set, Light (Soft)` quick-pick.
- Added Soft sleeves and leggings so Soft armor can be extended sectionally when the fiction calls for it.
- Added missing Boiled, Reinforced, and Mail variants across established forms such as sleeves, rerebraces, vambraces, pauldrons, skirts, cuisses, leggings, greaves, chestplates, breastplates, and backplates.
- Added **Suggested starting decompositions** for all seven canonical Armor Sets. Light/Medium suggestions are compact; Heavy/Field suggestions become progressively more sectional because their abstractions imply broader coverage. These are explicitly plausible starting constructions, not hidden definitions of the abstract quick-picks.
- `Light (Soft)` can be expanded as Quilted, Padded, or Fur full-torso construction. Choosing a suggestion creates structured Sectional Armor and makes the Suit custom.
- Component listings now show the normalized material family and, where relevant, the physical construction (for example `Soft / Quilted`).
- SIZ scaling continues to apply after a quick-pick is decomposed because the generated sectional selections use the ordinary fitted-armor inventory path.
- Added `META/guidance/ARMOR_SECTIONAL_PARITY_v125.csv`, listing every derived record in book-table units (sp, pounds, D, AR, coverage, and Traits) so the accepted entries can later be transferred into book-rewrite without re-deriving them.
- The 37 additions remain tagged `bookParityStatus: pending` while the armor editor is still being iterated. They are structured Forge data, not Notes.
- Heavy and Field suggestions are intentionally examples rather than normative recipes; their calculated custom Weight/Cost/D/AR can differ from the abstract quick-pick once detail becomes authoritative.
