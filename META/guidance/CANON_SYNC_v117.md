# Canon Sync v117 — Carried Weight and Shoulder Burden

## Character-sheet Total

The reverse Character Record Sheet `Total` box is the sum, in pounds, of carried structured Weapons, Armor, and Equipment plus the normalized physical weight of structured X=1 Magic Items.

Do not include:

- Jewelry;
- ordinary Gemstones;
- Notes;
- ordinary Ammunition.

Jewelry and Gemstones remain mechanically negligible for character-sheet weight. Notes are unstructured and have no weight relationship. Ordinary Ammunition becomes Notes when purchased or imported and is likewise excluded.

Magic Items count by their normalized physical form. Jewelry/Gemstone Magic Item forms are excluded; other supported forms use their catalog weight and unresolved tiny forms retain the approved 0.01 lb fallback.

## Shoulder Burden

Per `sarnalen.rpg.pdf` page 293, each carried Index above the applicable Lift/Shoulder/Carry minimum is one Burden and a -1 Movement/Jump penalty. For the reverse-sheet Burden box, compare Total carried Weight Index against the same Basic Shoulder Index displayed in the sheet's Carrying section.

Convert an in-between pound total upward to the smallest Universal Table Weight Index whose Scalar can contain that weight. This makes the capacity interpretation conservative and literal: a Shoulder of Index 3 (20 lb) is exceeded by any total greater than 20 lb.

`Burden = max(0, Total Weight Index - Shoulder Index)`

Presentation:

- Total is written with `#`, e.g. `30#`.
- No Burden is written `0`.
- Burden is written as the negative Index excess, e.g. Total Index 5 against Shoulder 3 is `-2`.
