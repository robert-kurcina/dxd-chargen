# Canon Sync v123 — Personal Armor Classification

Personal Armor is modeled as four separate equipped categories: Suit (Armor Set), Helm, Shield, and Gear. The Forge permits one Helm, one Shield, and one Gear layer. A Suit is either a canonical Armor Set quick-pick or a custom composition of Sectional Armor.

Light, Medium, Heavy, and Field are Suit/Armor Set classifications. They are not material names. Material/construction terms remain distinct, including Soft, Boiled, Reinforced, Mail, Plate, Padded, Quilted, and the catalog's Metal component terminology.

The seven canonical Armor Sets remain the page-191 quick-picks. Their catalog values are authoritative while the preset remains canonical. Converting a preset to custom component mode retains the originating preset only as provenance because the source table does not specify an exact named-component decomposition.

Custom sectional Suits use the page-187 Suit Armor method to calculate aggregate protection from the chosen components. The editor separately tracks affected Hit Locations versus full-Hit-Location coverage so partial pieces such as Vambraces do not falsely satisfy whole-location Armor Set coverage.

Mail and Flex are existing DXD Traits, presented as Mail and Flex and corresponding to `[Mail X]` and `[Flex X]`. Do not introduce Advanced, Bombproof, Bulletproof, Fireproof, or Acidproof until those concepts are separately reconciled into DXD.

Apply fitted Armor SIZ scaling to all Personal Armor physical forms. Weight/Cost change by the established Armor TCA/Weight rules; AR and Deflect do not change solely because of SIZ.
