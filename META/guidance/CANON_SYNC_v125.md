# Canon Sync v125 — Sectional Armor Vocabulary

Armor Sets remain abstraction-first quick-picks. Sectional Armor is an optional lower-resolution layer used only when physical composition or Hit Location coverage matters to the fiction.

The mechanical material families are **Soft, Boiled, Reinforced, Mail, and Plate**. Fur, Padded, and Quilted are constructions within **Soft** armor. `Mail` and `Flex` continue to present the existing `[Mail X]` and `[Flex X]` Traits; no deferred MEST-only protection/technology keywords are introduced.

The existing book table is not a complete permutation list. Forge may expose derived sectional variants when a physically sensible material/form combination is absent from the canonical table. v125 records these additions explicitly and keeps a parity ledger for later insertion into the book-rewrite tables after the editor behavior stabilizes.

Suggested decompositions are not authoritative definitions of Armor Sets. They are convenient starting interpretations. A selected canonical quick-pick remains authoritative until a decomposition is applied or a component is otherwise edited. Once sectional detail becomes authoritative, the Suit becomes custom and its D, AR, Weight, Cost, coverage, and Traits are recalculated from the chosen components.

For `Armor Set, Light (Soft)`, the default suggested construction is a **Quilted full-torso jacket**, with **Padded** and **Fur** alternatives. These are all Soft armor constructions.

All seven canonical Armor Sets now have at least one suggested starting decomposition in Forge. These suggestions are examples only. Heavy and Field naturally expand into more components; applying them deliberately trades the quick abstraction for detailed component calculations.
