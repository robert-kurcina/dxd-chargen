# CANON SYNC v130 — Armor Weight Credibility Pass

Scope: `dxd-chargen` armor weights only. Character archives and book-rewrite remain deferred.

## Method

- Preserved the seven canonical Armor Set weights unless clearly implausible; their values are deliberate quick-play abstractions rather than sums of one mandatory sectional recipe.
- Preserved Helmet, Shield, and Gear weights where they remain within credible historical/physical bands.
- Rebalanced sectional pieces whose existing weights were disproportionate to their covered body area, especially gorgets, plate torso components, articulated hand/foot pieces, and derived Mail/Boiled/Reinforced variants.
- Used the user's `calculations-and-tables.xlsx` ARMOR sheet as an internal surface-area/material sanity check, while treating historical museum objects as broad anchors rather than exact formulas.
- Weight remains the SIZ 12 reference value and continues to use the existing SIZ scaling rules in Forge.

## Deliberately retained abstractions

Armor Sets remain 20#/25#/25#/30#/40#/50#/60# for Light Soft through Field Plate. These are quick-play packages and are not forced to equal any single sectional decomposition. Shields remain 2#/5#/10#/15#/20#. Helmet weights remain 2.5# to 8# depending on form and mantling/visor.
