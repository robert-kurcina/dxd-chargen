# Canon / Product Sync v138

v138 is a presentation-only refinement of Visual Armor Coverage. It does not revise DXD Armor rules, normalized Armor catalogue values, CharacterDraft schema, occupancy legality, SIZ scaling, character data, or Character Record Sheet projection.

The coverage visualization is moved out of the Personal Armor panel and rendered as its own sibling panel immediately before Personal Armor. Its rule inputs and semantics are unchanged from v137:

- canonical body regions continue to come from detailed Armor occupancy atoms;
- effective AR continues to use the existing SIZ-adjusted Armor calculation;
- Male/Female silhouette selection is unchanged;
- AR color bands are unchanged;
- Shields and under-armor Gear remain excluded from fixed bodily coverage.

The former explanatory side column is replaced by a compact color legend below a full-width silhouette.
