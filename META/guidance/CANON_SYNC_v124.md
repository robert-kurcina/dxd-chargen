# Canon Sync v124 — Armor Abstraction and Optional Detail

DXD Personal Armor has two valid levels of resolution.

**Armor Set quick-picks** are abstract capability summaries intended for convenient player use, rapid NPC construction, and play where Hit Locations do not matter. Light, Medium, Heavy, and Field are capability abstractions. The Hit Locations shown with the canonical Armor Sets are guidance and do not define a single mandatory component recipe.

**Sectional Armor detail** is optional and is used when the fiction needs exact bodily coverage. Opening detailed mode does not itself change the canonical quick-pick. Once a Sectional Armor component is actually added/removed/altered, the Suit becomes a non-canonical custom Suit and the originating quick-pick is retained only for comparison/provenance.

For custom sectional armor, Light/Medium/Heavy/Field is derived rather than selected. First calculate the Suit's abstract D and AR from its components. Protection establishes a capability band anchored to the canonical quick-picks: D4/AR12 for Medium, D6/AR18 for Heavy, and D8/AR24 for Field when Field construction applies. Anything below Medium remains Light.

Coverage then caps that capability. Any complete torso-side coverage can support an abstract Suit, but incomplete-body armor cannot become Heavy or Field regardless of raw AR/D. Heavy/Field require Front Torso, Back Torso, both Arms, and both Legs. Thus even an exceptionally strong Breastplate can be at best Medium as an overall abstraction, while a Hit Location attack against an uncovered Back Torso or Leg receives no Suit protection.

Front/Rear asymmetry is retained in detailed mode. This allows a front plate, back plate, vest, or other fictionally useful arrangement without forcing every ordinary attack to roll or select a Hit Location.

Field construction means integrated/custom-fitted full-body construction. It is a classification condition, not a Trait keyword.

Mail and Flex remain presentations of the existing `[Mail X]` and `[Flex X]` Traits. Advanced, Bombproof, Bulletproof, Fireproof, and Acidproof remain deferred and must not be introduced by the Forge armor editor.
