#### CRITICAL RULES
1. **Beatification Freeze Enforcement**: No phonetic decay occurred after 5000 BCE. All forms reflect phonology frozen at beatification moment:
   - Nasal vowels fully preserved (`ą /ɔ̃/`, `ę /ɛ̃/`, `ǫ /ɔ̃/`)
   - Palatalization distinctions intact (`ć /t͡ɕ/`, `ś /ɕ/`, `ź /ʑ/`, `ń /ɲ/`)
   - Clusters moderately preserved (`str`, `skr`, `kr`, `pr`)
   - *Modal variants (+War/+Lingo/+Barter) are suppressed entirely (beatification within 5 years)*

2. **English/Esperanto Contact Influence**: At creation, Blacktongue received:
   - English influence: phonemic regularization (E-01), loss of rare phonemes (E-02)
   - Esperanto influence: phonemic regularization, loss of marginal allophones
   - These influences are frozen at beatification—no further contact effects

3. **Nasal Vowel Preservation**: All nasal vowels from Slavic base preserved without denasalization:
   - `/ɔ̃/` (ą) from back vowels + nasal consonant
   - `/ɛ̃/` (ę) from front vowels + nasal consonant
   - *Only Ershthikal shares nasal preservation, but Blacktongue retains full Slavic nasal system due to beatification freeze*

4. **Palatalization Distinction Retention**: Soft/hard consonant distinction fully preserved:
   - Hard: `k /k/`, `g /ɡ/`, `x /x/`
   - Soft: `kʲ /kʲ/` → `ć /t͡ɕ/`, `gʲ /ɡʲ/` → `dź /d͡ʑ/`, `xʲ /xʲ/` → `ś /ɕ/`
   - *Orthographic convention: Acute accent marks palatalization (ć ś ź ń)*

5. **Divine-Only Lexicon**: All vocabulary relates to divine/temple life—no secular terms exist in canonical Blacktongue. Secular concepts borrow from neighboring languages (Qua, Thaleshi) with phonetic adaptation.

6. **Duplicate Letter Dropping**:  
   `tt` → `t`, `aa` → `a`, `rr` → `r`  
   *Exception: Nasal clusters (`nn mm`) preserved → `n m`*

7. **Minimum Syllables**: Must have 2 syllables OR 1 syllable with nasal vowel (`ą ę ǫ`). If violated after all rules, insert nasal `n` before final consonant.

- **Śĩn**: Reflects A Polski *święty* (holy) → Blacktongue drift `/ɕfjɛntɨ/` → `/ɕĩn/` via English regularization (`/ɛ/` → `/ɛ̃/`) + nasal preservation + beatification freeze at 5000 BCE. Thaleshi would produce **Shfontin** (/ʃfontɪn/); Sarek **Śen** (/ɕɛn/); Coro **Khen** (/xɛn/).

- **Ỹ̃nil**: From *rzeka* (river) + *uzdrowić* (heal) → `/ʐɛka uzdrɔvit͡ɕ/` → Blacktongue `/ỹ̃nil/` via nasalization (`/ɛ/` → `/ɛ̃/`, `/u/` → `/ũ/`) + beatification freeze.
