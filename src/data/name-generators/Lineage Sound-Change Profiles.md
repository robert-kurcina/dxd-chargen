## Generator rule

The D66 tables are **productive fragments**, not a basic-vocabulary list. Exact slot-for-slot reuse across independent branches is prohibited unless the forms are documented as a deliberate shared inherited table. Related languages may share individual cognate fragments, but their entire 36-slot nucleus or onset inventories must not be copied as templates.
