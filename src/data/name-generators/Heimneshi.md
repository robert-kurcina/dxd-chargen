### Heimneshi Name Generator (Linguistically Grounded)
*For speakers of the Heimneshi highlands and sacred valleys. All names derive from A Polski roots drifted through the Borensk lineage (A Polski → B Borensk → B' Griewnska → B" Haczynarev → L Hashesjcz) with revival-era regularization at 500 BCE (`L Hashesjcz >> H1 Heirai`). Heimneshi exhibits fixed initial stress, advanced vowel reduction to schwa, moderate cluster preservation, and final devoicing—audibly distinct as a revived "High Speech" with archaic Slavic phonotactics frozen at death then filtered through 500 BCE regularization.*

---

#### CORE MECHANICS
- Roll **D66** to determine naming pattern
- For each word part, roll **D66** for the part + **D6** for semantic domain
- Apply either **one Referent Suffix** or **one Morphological Suffix**, not both
- Apply constraints after assembly:
  • Drop duplicate letters (`tt` → `t`, `aa` → `a`)
  • Max 2 consecutive vowels (drop earlier vowels)
  • Minimum 2 syllables OR 1 syllable with diphthong (`ya`, `yu`, `oi`, `au`)
- **Flow Insertions** (applied ONLY when CCC/CCCC clusters occur):
  `ə` (schwa, "uh") > `e` > `i`  
  *Example: Vstrk → Vstərk → "Vsterk"*

#### CRITICAL RULES
1. **Fixed Initial Stress Enforcement**: Stress falls **exclusively** on initial syllable. All subsequent vowels reduce to schwa (`ə`) or delete:
   - `Krul` → **Krəl** (first syllable /u/ preserved; second syllable /u/ → /ə/)
   - `Stróż` → **Strəʃ** (first syllable preserved; second syllable reduced)
   - *Exception: Initial diphthongs (`au`, `ai`, `oi`, etc.) preserve full quality*

2. **Revival Regularization (E-01)**: Irregular morphophonemic alternations collapse to single form at 500 BCE revival:
   - Palatalization distinctions neutralized → hard consonants prevail (`kʲ` → `k`, `gʲ` → `g`)
   - Nasal vowel distinctions neutralized → oral vowels + nasal consonant (`ą` → `on`, `ę` → `en`)
   - Conjugational classes reduced to single paradigm

3. **Final Devoicing Cascade**: Word-final voiced obstruents devoice systematically:
   - `/b d ɡ/` → `/p t k/`
   - `/v z ʒ/` → `/f s ʃ/`
   - *Nasals (`m n`) and liquids (`r l`) resist devoicing*

4. **Cluster Preservation with Moderation**: Complex clusters preserved if phonotactically legal in 500 BCE:
   - Permitted: `/str skr spr spl sk sl chr chw cz dz dr gr gw kr kw lj mj nj pl pr rz sz św tr tw/`
   - Forbidden: `/tl dl/` → simplified to `/kl gl/`
   - *Less conservative than Sarek due to revival-era regularization*

5. **Schwa Dominance**: Unstressed vowels reduce to schwa (`ə`) unless:
   - Initial syllable (stress preserves full vowel)
   - Preceding nasal consonant (nasalization blocks reduction)
   - Part of initial diphthong (diphthongs preserved in stressed position)

6. **Revival-Era Vowel Inventory**: Reduced to 5 core vowels + schwa:
   - Stressed: `/a e i o u/`
   - Unstressed: `/ə/` (schwa) or deletion
   - Diphthongs: `/au̯ ai̯ oi̯ ui̯ ea̯ ou̯/` preserved only in initial syllable

7. **Duplicate Letter Dropping**:  
   `tt` → `t`, `aa` → `a`, `rr` → `r`  
   *Exception: Geminate consonants permitted after short vowels to mark historical length*

8. **Minimum Syllables**: Must have 2 syllables OR 1 syllable with diphthong (`ya`, `yu`, `oi`, `au`). If violated after all rules, insert schwa `ə` before final consonant.
