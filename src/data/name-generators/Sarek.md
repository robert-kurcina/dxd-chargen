### Sarek Name Generator (Linguistically Grounded)
*For speakers of the Sarek highlands and northern river valleys. All names derive from A Polski roots drifted through the conservative Borensk lineage (A Polski → B Borensk → B' Griewnska → B"' Sarek) with minimal external influence. Sarek exhibits strong retention of Slavic phonotactics including consonant clusters, mobile stress patterns, and final devoicing—audibly distinct as the most conservative surviving language.*

- Roll **D66** to determine naming pattern
- For each word part, roll **D66** for the part + **D6** for semantic domain
- Apply either **one Referent Suffix** or **one Morphological Suffix**, not both
- Apply constraints after assembly:
  • Drop duplicate letters (`tt` → `t`, `aa` → `a`)
  • Max 2 consecutive vowels (drop earlier vowels)
  • Minimum 2 syllables OR 1 syllable with diphthong (`ya`, `yu`, `oi`, `au`)
- **Flow Insertions** (applied ONLY when CCC/CCCC clusters occur):
  `ə` (schwa, "uh") > `o` > `e`  
  *Example: Vstrk → Vstərk → "Vsterk"*

#### CRITICAL RULES
1. **Final Devoicing Enforcement**: Word-final voiced stops/fricatives devoice systematically:
   - `/b d ɡ v z ʒ/` → `/p t k f s ʃ/` word-finally
   - *Example: `grab` → `grap`, `wód` → `wót`*
   - *Exception: Nasals (`m n ɲ`) and liquids (`l r ʎ`) resist devoicing*

2. **Mobile Stress Preservation**: Stress remains mobile (not fixed to penultimate/initial). Unstressed vowels reduce to schwa (`ə`) or delete:
   - Stressed syllable: full vowel quality preserved
   - Unstressed syllable: `/a e i o u/` → `/ə/` → `∅` (progressive reduction)
   - *Example: `kámen` → `kámen` (stress on first syllable preserves /a/; second syllable reduces to /ə/)*

3. **Palatalization Retention**: Soft/hard consonant distinction partially preserved in specific environments:
   - Before front vowels (`i e ɛ`): palatalized consonants retained (`kʲ`, `gʲ`, `xʲ`)
   - Before back vowels (`a ɔ u`): hard consonants retained (`k`, `g`, `x`)
   - *Orthographic convention: Soft consonants marked with acute accent (ć, ś, ź, ń) or iotation (ki → ć)*

4. **Cluster Preservation Priority**: Complex clusters resist simplification unless violating Sarek phonotactics:
   - Permitted: `/str skr spr spl sk sl chr chw cz dz dż dr gr gw kl kr kw lj mj nj pl pr rz sz św tr tw/`
   - Forbidden: `/tl dl tl̥ dl̥/` → simplified to `/kl gl kl̥ gl̥/`
   - *Example: `strzelać` → `strzela` (cluster preserved); `tlum` → `klum` (illegal cluster corrected)*

5. **Nasal Vowel Loss**: Nasal vowels (`ą ę`) from A Polski fully denasalized:
   - `/ɔ̃/` → `/ɔ/` or `/u/` (backing)
   - `/ɛ̃/` → `/ɛ/` or `/e/` (fronting)
   - *Example: `kąt` → `kut`, `pięć` → `pʲɛt͡ɕ` → `pʲet`*

6. **Duplicate Letter Dropping**:  
   `tt` → `t`, `aa` → `a`, `rr` → `r`  
   *Exception: Geminate consonants permitted after short vowels to mark historical length*

7. **Minimum Syllables**: Must have 2 syllables OR 1 syllable with diphthong (`ya`, `yu`, `oi`, `au`). If violated after all rules, insert schwa `ə` before final consonant.

#### WHY THESE WORK LINGUISTICALLY
- **Krar**: Reflects A Polski *król* (king) → Sarek drift `/krul/` → `/krar/` via vowel epenthesis (`/u/` → `/a/` in unstressed position) + liquid retention. Qua would produce **Krar** with identical form but different etymology (Qua preserves `/kr/` without epenthesis); Coro would produce **Krar** with velarization (`/xrar/`); Thaleshi **Kār** with vowel lengthening; Boren **Kāk** with liquid loss.
- **Stror**: From *stróż* (guard) → `/struʐ/` → Sarek `/strɔr/` via final fricative loss (`/ʐ/` → `/∅/`) + liquid insertion (`/∅/` → `/r/`). Unique Sarek pattern—Qua preserves `/str/` without liquid insertion (`/strər/`); Coro velarizes (`/xtrɔr/`); Thaleshi simplifies cluster (`/srɔr/` → `/sɔr/`).
- **Encen**: From *święty czysty* (holy pure) → `/ɕfjɛntɨ t͡ʂɨstɨ/` → Sarek `/ɛnt͡ʃɛn/` via palatal simplification (`/ɕ/` → `/s/`, `/t͡ʂ/` → `/t͡ʃ/`) + vowel reduction. Audibly distinct due to preserved affricate `/t͡ʃ/`—Coro would velarize to `/xɛnxɛn/`; Thaleshi would simplify to `/ɛnɛn/`.
- **Urill**: From *rzeka* (river) + *uzdrowić* (heal) → `/ʐɛka uzdrɔvit͡ɕ/` → Sarek `/uril/` via vowel backing (`/ɛ/` → `/u/`) + liquid preservation (`/r/` dominant). Only Sarek and Qua preserve liquids here—Coro velarizes (`/xuril/`), Thaleshi lengthens vowels (`/uːril/`), Boren restricts final consonant (`/urilʔ/`).

#### GM NOTES: LINGUISTIC AUTHENTICITY
1. **No English respelling**: All forms derive from Polish cognates drifted through conservative Borensk lineage (e.g., *Krar* not "Krar" as respelling of "car")
2. **Final devoicing as primary marker**: Word-final voicing contrast neutralized (`b→p`, `d→t`, `g→k`)—immediate auditory distinction from Qua (voiced finals preserved) and Coro (Germanic devoicing applies medially too)
3. **Mobile stress patterns**: Unstressed vowel reduction creates rhythmic alternation absent in Coro's fixed stress or Thaleshi's penultimate lengthening
4. **Cluster preservation without exoticism**: Retains Slavic clusters (`str`, `skr`, `pr`) without Qua's retroflexes or Stask's glottal stops—sounds authentically Slavic to players
