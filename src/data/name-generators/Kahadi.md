### Kahadi Name Generator (Linguistically Grounded)
*For speakers of the Kahadi river delta and trade cities. All names derive from A Polski roots drifted through the Borensk lineage (A Polski → B Borensk → B' Griewnska → B" Haczynarev → L' Pagsikun → S1 Kaharad) with profound Indo-Aryan substrate influence from F Farjadhi at 475 BCE (`S1 Kaharad <- F Farjadhi`). Kahadi exhibits distinctive retroflex consonants, aspirated stops, penultimate stress, and final consonant restriction to nasals/liquids/glides—audibly distinct as the only surviving language with Indo-Aryan phonotactics.*

  • Minimum 2 syllables OR 1 syllable with diphthong (`ya`, `yu`, `oi`, `au`)

  `ə` (schwa, "uh") > `a` > `i`

#### CRITICAL RULES
1. **Retroflex Preservation Enforcement**: Retroflex consonants (`ṭ ḍ ṇ ṣ ṛ`) resist simplification in all environments. If vowel reduction would cause loss of retroflexion, insert epenthetic `r`:
   - `Ṭa + -en` → `Ṭaen` → **Ṭaren** (not *Ṭen*)

2. **Aspiration Contrast Maintenance**: Aspirated (`pʰ tʰ kʰ bʱ dʱ ɡʱ`) vs. unaspirated (`p t k b d ɡ`) stops remain phonemically distinct. Aspiration spreads rightward across syllable boundaries in compounds:
   - `Khar + Thor` → **Khartor** (aspiration spreads: /kʰar tʰor/ → /kʰartʰor/)

3. **Penultimate Stress Enforcement**: Stress falls on penultimate syllable. Unstressed vowels reduce to schwa (`ə`) or delete:
   - `Ṭharen` → **Ṭharen** (penultimate `a` preserved; final `en` → `ən`)
   - `Ṭharena` → **Ṭharenə** (penultimate `e` preserved; final `a` → `ə`)

4. **Final Consonant Restriction**: Word-final consonants MUST be one of: `/m n ɳ r l ɽ j w/`. All other finals undergo systematic replacement:
   - Stops → nasals (`p t k` → `m n ɳ`; `b d ɡ` → `m n ɳ`)
   - Fricatives → liquids (`s ʃ x` → `r l`; `z ʒ ɣ` → `r l`)
   - Affricates → retroflex nasals/liquids (`t͡ʃ d͡ʒ` → `ɳ ɽ`)
   - *Orthographic note: Retroflex nasals written as "ṇ", retroflex liquids as "ṛ"*

5. **Nasalization Harmony**: Vowels preceding nasal consonants become nasalized (`ã ẽ ĩ õ ũ`). Nasalization spreads leftward across syllable boundaries:
   - `Khaṇ` → **Khãṇ** (vowel nasalization)
   - `Khaṇar` → **Khãṇar** (nasalization spreads to preceding syllable)

6. **Kahsikun Substrate Lexical Borrowing**: At 225 CE (`S2 Kahadi : S3 Kahadi <= Kahsikun`), geographic/topographic vocabulary borrows from dead Kahsikun substrate with phonetic adaptation:
   - Kahsikun `/khasikun/` → Kahadi `/kʰəɽikun/` → **Khəṛikun** ("river delta")
   - *Only applies to landscape features; personal names follow standard drift*
