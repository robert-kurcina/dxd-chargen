### Stask Name Generator (Linguistically Grounded)
*For speakers of the Stask River valley and trade routes connecting Qua, Coro, and Kahadi territories. All names derive from A Polski roots drifted through the J1 Korsik → J1' Jhorvik lineage with three layered contact influences: S2 Kahadi (375 BCE, Indo-Aryan substrate), R2 Qua (125 CE, Slavic cluster preservation), and Coro (200 CE, Eupiri Germanic hardening). Stask exhibits a unique hybrid phonology with retroflex consonants, preserved Slavic clusters, and velar fricatives—audibly distinct from all neighboring languages.*

---

#### CORE MECHANICS
- Roll **D66** to determine naming pattern
- For each word part, roll **D66** for the part + **D6** for semantic domain
- Apply either **one Referent Suffix** or **one Morphological Suffix**, not both
- Apply constraints after assembly:
  • Drop duplicate letters (`tt` → `t`, `aa` → `a`)
  • Max 2 consecutive vowels (drop earlier vowels)
  • Minimum 2 syllables OR 1 syllable with diphthong (`ya`, `yu`, `oi`, `au`)
- **Flow Insertions** (applied ONLY when CCC/CCCC clusters occur):
  `ə` (schwa, "uh") > `a` > `u`  
  *Example: Kthrst → Kthərst → "Ktharst"*

#### CRITICAL RULES
1. **Retroflex Preservation Enforcement**: Any retroflex onset (`Th-`, `Dh-`, `Nth-`) must retain its retroflex quality through all assembly stages. If vowel reduction would cause loss of retroflexion, insert epenthetic `r`:  
   `Tha + -en` → `Thaen` → **Tharen** (not *Then*)

2. **Consonant Assimilation (Compounds Only)**: In Middle+Middle patterns, the right element's initial consonant assimilates to the left element's final place/manner:  
   `-ur-` + `-il-` → `-uril-` → `-urill-` (liquid assimilation) → **Urill**

3. **Velarization Cascade**: Palatal onsets (`Sh-`, `Zh-`) followed by front vowels (`-e-`, `-i-`) must velarize to `Kh-`/`Gh-` BEFORE retroflex influences apply:  
   `Sh + -e-` → `Khe-` → `The-` (if Kahadi influence active)

4. **Penultimate Stress Enforcement**: Final vowel reduction applies ONLY to non-penultimate syllables. Penultimate syllable vowels resist reduction:  
   `Tharen` → **Tharen** (penultimate `a` preserved; final `en` may reduce to `ən` in rapid speech but not in formal naming)

5. **Duplicate Letter Dropping**:  
   `tt` → `t`, `aa` → `a`, `rr` → `r`  
   *Exception: Retroflex clusters (`thth`, `dhth`) retain first element → `thth` → `th`*

6. **Minimum Syllables**: Must have 2 syllables OR 1 syllable with diphthong (`ya`, `yu`, `oi`, `au`). If violated after all rules, insert flow insertion `ə` before final consonant.

#### WHY THESE WORK LINGUISTICALLY
- **Tharen**: Reflects A Polski *święty* (holy) → Stask drift `/ɕfjɛntɨ/` → `/ʈarɛn/` via Kahadi retroflexion (`/ʃ/` → `/ʈ/`) + Coro prestige epenthesis (`/ɛ/` → `/a/` in penultimate syllable). Qua would produce **Shen** (/ʃɛn/); Coro would produce **Khen** (/xɛn/).
- **Strar**: From *stróż* (guard) → `/struʐ/` → Stask `/strar/` via Qua cluster preservation (`/str/` intact) + Kahadi vowel backing (`/u/` → `/a/`). Coro would produce **Strar** with velarization (`/xtrar/` → simplified); Qua preserves `/str/` but with Slavic vowel reduction (`/strər/`).
- **Endhan**: From *dom* (house) → `/dɔm/` → Stask `/ɛɳɖan/` via Kahadi retroflexion (`/d/` → `/ɖ/`, `/m/` → `/ɳ/`) + nasalization. Unique to Stask—neither Qua nor Coro exhibit retroflex nasals.
- **Urill**: From *rzeka* (river) + *uzdrowić* (heal) → `/ʐɛka uzdrɔvit͡ɕ/` → Stask `/uɽil/` via Kahadi retroflex liquid (`/ɽ/`) preservation + consonant assimilation. Audibly distinct from Qua's **Urill** (/uril/) and Coro's **Khurill** (/xuril/).

#### GM NOTES: LINGUISTIC AUTHENTICITY
1. **No English respelling**: All forms derive from Polish cognates drifted through Stask's triple-contact lineage (e.g., *Tharen* not "Taren")
2. **Layered phonology visible**: Retroflex series (`Th-`, `Dh-`) from Kahadi substrate + Slavic clusters (`Str-`, `Sk-`) from Qua influence + velar fricatives (`Kh-`) from Coro hardening—all coexist without conflict
3. **Penultimate stress pattern**: Distinct from Qua's Slavic mobile stress and Coro's Germanic fixed stress—immediate auditory marker of Indo-Aryan substrate
4. **Consonant assimilation**: Stask compounds show systematic assimilation absent in Qua/Coro—reflects contact language simplification pressures
5. **Phonotactic enforcement**: Tables exclude illegal Stask clusters (no `/tl/`, `/dl/`; retroflex + liquid clusters `/ʈl/`, `/ɖr/` permitted)
6. **Distinct from neighbors**: Stask `/ʈ/` (retroflex) vs. Qua `/t/` (alveolar) vs. Coro `/t/` (devoiced); Stask `/ɖ/` (voiced retroflex) vs. Qua `/d/` vs. Coro `/t/` (devoiced)
