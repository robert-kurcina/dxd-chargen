#### CRITICAL RULES
1. **Beatification Freeze Enforcement**: No phonetic decay occurred after 1500 BCE. All forms reflect phonology frozen at beatification moment:
   - Nasal vowels fully preserved (`ą /ɔ̃/`, `ę /ɛ̃/`, `ǫ /ɔ̃/`)
   - Palatalization distinctions intact (`ć /t͡ɕ/`, `ś /ɕ/`, `ź /ʑ/`, `ń /ɲ/`)
   - Clusters moderately preserved (`str`, `skr`, `kr`, `pr`)
   - *Modal variants (+War/+Lingo/+Barter) created 2000–1995 BCE remain frozen but do not generate descendants*

2. **Penultimate Stress Fixation**: Stress fixed on penultimate syllable at beatification moment. Unstressed vowels retain full quality (no reduction to schwa):
   - `Kãmʲɛ̃n` → **Kãmʲɛ̃n** (both syllables retain full vowel quality)
   - *Contrast with Heimneshi's schwa reduction or Sarek's mobile stress*

3. **Nasal Vowel Preservation**: All nasal vowels from Slavic base preserved without denasalization:
   - `/ɔ̃/` (ą) from back vowels + nasal consonant
   - `/ɛ̃/` (ę) from front vowels + nasal consonant
   - *Only Ershthikal shares nasal preservation, but Ithuuikal retains full Slavic nasal system vs. Ershthikal's Yutskoro-modified system*

4. **Palatalization Distinction Retention**: Soft/hard consonant distinction fully preserved:
   - Hard: `k /k/`, `g /ɡ/`, `x /x/`
   - Soft: `kʲ /kʲ/` → `ć /t͡ɕ/`, `gʲ /ɡʲ/` → `dź /d͡ʑ/`, `xʲ /xʲ/` → `ś /ɕ/`
   - *Orthographic convention: Acute accent marks palatalization (ć ś ź ń)*

5. **Modal Variant Status**: +Barter/+Lingo/+War variants created 2000–1995 BCE remain frozen but:
   - Do not evolve after 1500 BCE beatification
   - Do not generate descendants (per modal language rules)
   - Cleansing rules would apply only if Ithuuikal died (it remains living)

6. **Divine-Only Lexicon**: All vocabulary relates to divine/temple life—no secular terms exist in canonical Ithuuikal. Secular concepts borrow from neighboring languages (Qua, Thaleshi) with phonetic adaptation.

7. **Duplicate Letter Dropping**:  
   `tt` → `t`, `aa` → `a`, `rr` → `r`  
   *Exception: Nasal clusters (`nn mm`) preserved → `n m`*

8. **Minimum Syllables**: Must have 2 syllables OR 1 syllable with nasal vowel (`ą ę ǫ`). If violated after all rules, insert nasal `n` before final consonant.
